# AFAQ SUPPLY — Design System Specifications

## 1. Brand Identity & Visual Tokens
- **Brand Wordmark:** `AFAQ SUPPLY` (Secondary: `AFAQ`)
- **Tagline:** `THE ART OF EVERYDAY STYLE.`
- **Industry:** Editorial streetwear, luxury essentials, and archival footwear.
- **Design Philosophy:** Minimalist luxury, spacious proportions, uncrowded compositions, and restrained micro-interactions.

---

## 3. Typography Architecture

### Headings: Playfair Display
- Font Family: `var(--font-serif)`
- Weight: `400` (Regular) / `500` (Medium)
- Letter Spacing: Tight tracking (`-0.03em`) on large display titles, wide tracking (`0.25em`) on uppercase tags.

### Body & Navigation: Inter / Geist
- Font Family: `var(--font-sans)`
- Weight: `400` (Body) / `500` (Labels) / `600` (Buttons)

### Metadata & Pricing: Geist Mono
- Font Family: `var(--font-mono)`
- Format: `$199.00`, `01 / 07`, `EU 42`
